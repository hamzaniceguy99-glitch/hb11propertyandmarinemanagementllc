Our services, in detail

Four services for waterfront owners, reported in photographs after every visit.

Program 01 · Eyes on it while you are away

Property care & inspection

A closed-up house in Florida develops problems quietly: air conditioning failing and humidity rising, a slow leak, pests finding a way in. Regular visits catch those while they are small, and you get photographs rather than a reassurance that everything is fine.

  • Weekly or monthly
  • Absentee owners
  • Quoted in writing
  • Access and alarm codes

Program 02 · Where salt wins slowly

Docks, lifts, pilings & seawalls

Lift cables, bunk carpet, piling wraps and seawall caps all fail in predictable ways and on a predictable timescale. We monitor them, flag what is approaching the end of its life, and bring in licensed marine contractors before a repair becomes a rebuild.

  • Scheduled
  • Waterfront owners
  • Quoted in writing
  • Water access

Program 03 · A boat that starts

Vessel husbandry

Boats deteriorate fastest sitting still. Regular wash-downs stop salt doing permanent damage, and someone checking batteries, bilge pumps and through-hulls on a schedule means the vessel is usable when you arrive rather than the start of a week of repairs.

  • Weekly or monthly
  • Vessel owners
  • Quoted in writing
  • Vessel access

Program 04 · Agreed in May, not in August

Storm preparation & response

Storm plans made during a watch are made badly. We agree in writing beforehand what gets shuttered, what gets moved, whether the vessel is hauled or stripped, and who authorises it — then execute on warning and document the condition immediately afterwards for your insurer.

  • Seasonal
  • Waterfront owners
  • Quoted in writing
  • Advance authority
