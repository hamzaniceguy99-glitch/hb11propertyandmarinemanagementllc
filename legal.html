What we provide

The company provides caretaking, inspection, monitoring and coordination services. It is not a licensed general, marine, electrical or plumbing contractor and does not perform work requiring a licence; dock, seawall, lift, electrical and vessel repair work is carried out by appropriately licensed contractors engaged on the owner's behalf.
